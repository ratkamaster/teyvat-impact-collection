📜 Controls & Gameplay Guide

WASD Move character in 3D world
Mouse Look Orbit camera (Click canvas to lock cursor)
Left Click Normal Attack Combo (3-hit chain with elemental finisher)
Right Click / Shift Sprint / Dash (Consumes stamina, grants i-frames)
Space Jump / Deploy Wind Glider while in air
E Elemental Skill (Specific to active character)
Q Elemental Burst (Consumes energy for massive AoE)
1, 2, 3, 4 Switch party members (Pyro, Hydro, Electro, Anemo)
F Interact with Chests, Waypoints, Anemoculus
G Open Wish Banner (Pull for 5-star characters & weapons!)
Reactions Vaporize (Pyro+Hydro), Overload (Pyro+Electro), Swirl (Anemo+Any)
